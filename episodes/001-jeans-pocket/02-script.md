### Recording guide

- **Room:** small, soft room (curtains, cupboard of clothes). No tiled bathrooms.
- **Mic:** 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly off-axis. Phone voice memo is OK for a scratch take.
- **Takes:** 3 full takes, then each beat separately ×2. Leave 2 s of silence before each.
- **Signature:** record «غريب… عجيب.» 5 times: a short pause after غريب, with عجيب landing low and warm.
- **File:** WAV 48 kHz, 24-bit, one file per take, named `001_vo_take01.wav`…
- **Timing:** after the first take I'll measure your real pace and adjust the
  timeline. These timecodes are estimates.
