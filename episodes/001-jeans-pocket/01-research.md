## How verification was done, and its limit

Sources were located with web search. **Direct page access to levistrauss.com,
docsteach.org and web.archive.org failed from this environment (DNS blocked)**, so
the "evidence" quotes for those pages come from the search engine's extraction
of those pages, not from my own read of them. Every row that depends on one is
therefore marked **NEEDS CLICK-THROUGH**: a person opens the URL, confirms the
quote word for word, and only then flips the status to APPROVED. Nothing goes on
screen before that.

Grades follow Bible §28: **ثابت** (established) · **الأرجح** (most likely) ·
**مختلف عليه** (contested) · **غير مؤكد** (unverified, not used).

### Corrections this ledger forces on the storyboard

1. **Old scene 2 is wrong twice.** The small pocket is on the **front right**, not the back. And its "1→5" numbering implies the small pocket was added fifth. **C5/C8/C9 say the reverse:** it was original, and the 1901 back-left pocket is the fifth. This flip can be the episode's twist.
2. **Scenes with the 1870s "man with hat" and European streets** use fabricated images. They get replaced with archive images (see D), or labelled «تصوّر توضيحي».
3. **The coin "common belief" (old scene 8)** is supported as a *later use* (C10). Script it as "people call it the coin pocket… but it started as a watch pocket".
