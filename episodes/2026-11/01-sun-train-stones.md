## The idea, challenged

| | idea | verdict |

| original | "Etihad Rail now reaches Dubai" | News. Every account will post it. Rejected. |
| alt 1 | Why the tracks are full of stones | Everyone has seen it, nobody has asked. **Chosen.** |
| alt 2 | Why trains don't need a steering wheel | Good, but no local twist. → backup bank |
| alt 3 | Why the train is quieter than the car | Hard to prove on screen. Dropped. |

The twist is what makes it ours. In Europe the enemy of the stones is snow.
**Here it is sand**, and Etihad Rail has machines that clear it off the line
the way other countries clear snow. That turns a global fact into a UAE
story. It's also the reason someone saves it.
