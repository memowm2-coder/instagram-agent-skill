## The month in one line

**November is the month the UAE moves.** The train reaches Dubai, the city
runs (Dubai Fitness Challenge), Sharjah reads (Book Fair), the flag goes up
(3 Nov), the desert season opens, and everyone shops (White Friday). Maryam
explains how to ride it, and why it is stranger than it looks.

### Why these slots
- **Sun 1:** we open with the train because it is the biggest UAE story of the
  autumn. Dubai's station opened 30 Sep. The Wednesday explainer then
  converts that curiosity into saves.
- **Tue 3:** Flag Day gets a respectful غريب عجيب. A gag on this day would be
  wrong.
- **Sun 22:** the marathon episode goes out on the morning of the Dubai Run.
- **Wed 25 → Fri 27:** we protect people first, then explain the psychology.

## Episode notes: the three anchors, challenged

The skill requires three better alternatives for every idea. These are the
calls for the anchors. Every other episode gets the same treatment at script
lock.

**Sun 1, train stones.**
Original: "Etihad Rail now reaches Dubai" (news, rejected).
Alternatives:
1. Why the tracks have stones.
2. Why trains don't have steering wheels.
3. Why the train is quieter than the car.

**Chosen: 1.** Everyone has seen the stones and nobody has asked. The
proposed twist: some high-speed lines use no stones at all (slab track).
Verify it.

**Sun 8, cabin lights.**
Original: "airplane facts" (a list, rejected).
Alternatives:
1. Why the lights go off when the plane lands at night.
2. Why the window shade has to be open.
3. Why plane windows are round.

**Chosen: 1, with 2 as the twist.** Script draft already exists in `shows.md`.

**Sun 15, Burj Khalifa.**
Original: "Burj Khalifa records" (Wikipedia, rejected).
Alternatives:
1. It was drawn from a desert flower.
2. Why it sways.
3. Who cleans the windows.

**Chosen: 1.** The proposed twist: people on the highest floors break their
Ramadan fast a few minutes later than people on the ground. This is the
strongest twist of the month. Verify it carefully, then we may decide to
**save it for Ramadan (February 2027)** and use the sway as this twist
instead. Maryam's call.

## Research and sources (what this plan stands on)

- **Etihad Rail:** Dubai's Al Yalayis station and Al Dhaid opened for
  passengers on 30 Sep 2026. The Abu Dhabi–Fujairah service has run since
  30 Jun. Reported one-way fare Dubai–Abu Dhabi from Dh39 (Comfort).
  [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/transport/etihad-rail-opens-dubai-station-fares-connections-and-what-passengers-need-to-know/),
  [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/09/29/first-look-inside-dubais-new-etihad-rail-station-on-track-to-transform-public-transport/),
  [Railway Journal](https://www.railjournal.com/passenger/main-line/etihad-rail-extends-passenger-service-to-dubai/).
  **Before Wed 4:** confirm fares, timetable and booking flow in the
  official Etihad Rail app.
- **Dubai Fitness Challenge:** 31 Oct–29 Nov 2026. Dubai Ride 1 Nov, SUP at
  Hatta 7–8 Nov, Dubai Run 22 Nov, Dubai Yoga 29 Nov.
  [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-fitness-challenge-2026-dates-announced),
  [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai-fitness-challenge-2026-dates-announced-1.500639620).
- **Sharjah International Book Fair:** 4–15 Nov 2026, Expo Centre Sharjah,
  Oman guest of honour.
  [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c13o6i2-45th-sharjah-international-book-fair-set-for),
  [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/books/2026/07/07/sharjah-international-book-fair-returns-in-november-with-oman-as-guest-of-honour/).
- **Dubai Design Week:** 3–8 Nov 2026, d3.
  [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=2297601).
  Used for visual reference and a possible collaboration, not an episode.
- **UAE Flag Day:** 3 Nov. The date is tied to the anniversary of Sheikh
  Khalifa's accession as President.
  [MOHAP](https://mohap.gov.ae/en/w/flag-day).
- **E-invoicing:** large businesses (AED 50m+ revenue) were to appoint an
  Accredited Service Provider by 30 Oct 2026 after an extension. Mandatory
  go-live is 1 Jan 2027. Smaller businesses follow from 1 Jul 2027. Reported
  penalty: AED 5,000 per month of delay.
  [ACCA](https://abmagazine.accaglobal.com/global/articles/2026/feb/practice/uae-s-e-invoicing-deadline-approaches.html),
  [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/uae-mandatory-e-invoicing-deadlines-announced/),
  [Fiscal Requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/5108-uae-e-invoicing-compliance-complete-2026-guide).
  **Dates have shifted once already. Confirm every date on the Ministry of
  Finance or FTA site.**
- **Visit visas:** reports describe new 30/60/90-day options and
  resident-sponsored visits.
  [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/life-and-living/visa-and-immigration-in-uae/uae-visa-rule-changes-2026).
  **Secondary sources only, and they conflict.** No episode ships until each
  option is confirmed on ICP or GDRFA.
- **National holidays:** Eid Al Etihad (the 55th) is expected on 2–3 Dec.
  Sources disagree on Commemoration Day (30 Nov vs 1 Dec).
  [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/06/02/uae-holiday-dates/).
  Hence the buffer slot.
- **December, for awareness:** GITEX Global moves to **7–11 Dec at Expo City**,
  so it is no longer an October/November topic
  ([Dubai Media Office](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/october/08-10/gitex-global-to-begin-a-new-chapter-at-expo-city-dubai-in-2026)).
  The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix is 3–6 Dec
  ([Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/abu-dhabi-grand-prix-2026-f1-confirms-december-race-dates)).
  `rituals.md` is updated to match.

**Not researched this round:** live Instagram/TikTok/YouTube performance in
the Gulf, Google Trends and Reddit. The research tools here can't read
platform data. Run `/ig-viral` with the team's own browser before the
