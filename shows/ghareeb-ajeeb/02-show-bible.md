> **كل شي عادي… وراه قصة غريبة.**

## PART A · WHO WE ARE

### 1. Brand Philosophy

**الغريب ساكن في المألوف.** *The strange lives inside the familiar.*

We don't go looking for weird things at the edge of the world. We take the
things you touched today (your phone, your ear, the oud in your majlis, the
word "ألو") and show you that you never actually looked at them.

Three beliefs:

1. **Wonder is a form of respect.** Looking closely at something ordinary
   honors it. We never mock what people believe. We look at it with them.
2. **Truth is more interesting than myth, and it should be shown, not just
   told.** If you can't see the answer on screen, we haven't finished the
   episode.
3. **Every fact is about a person.** Behind every strange thing is someone
   who invented, named, feared, built or loved it, and that's who we're really
   telling you about.

### 2. Mission

**نرجّع الدهشة للأشياء العادية، بمعلومة تقدر تتأكد منها.**
Bring wonder back to ordinary things, with information you can verify.

### 3. Vision

Within five years, **غريب عجيب is the most recognized and most trusted
short-form documentary brand in Arabic**, the one parents show their kids, and
teachers and museums ask to partner with.

Milestones that make the vision real, not decorative:

| Horizon | What exists |
| --- | --- |
| Season 1 (8 files) | A recognizable format. The mute-and-blur tests pass (section 31). |
| Year 1 | 3 seasons, a season trailer ritual, first presenting partner on our terms |
| Year 2 | The 3-minute "ملف كبير" format; YouTube/long-form cut; a live غريب عجيب segment at a summit Maryam hosts |
| Year 3–5 | The archive becomes an illustrated collage book; a museum or education partnership; a kids' edition |

### 4. Audience Psychology

**Primary:** Arabic-first viewers in the UAE and Gulf, 18–34, Khaleeji dialect,
curious but impatient, proud of where they come from.
**Secondary:** the wider Arab audience reached through identity and emotion.
**Tertiary:** parents who watch with their children.

What's happening in their heads, and what we do about it:

| Psychological driver | What it looks like | Our design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The curiosity gap** | "I've always done this and never asked why" | Every title is a "ليش" question about something they personally do |
| **Social currency** | They want something to *say* at the majlis, at dinner, in the family group | **The Majlis Test** (section 31): every episode must be retellable in two sentences |
| **Folk-belief loyalty** | "My grandmother said…" | We open with the belief respectfully, then investigate. We never ridicule it. |
| **Identity pride** | Outliers on this account are Arab/Emirati identity | One pillar is about *us*: our words, habits, objects, buildings |
| **Protection instinct** | "انتبهوا" was a top-3 outlier | Where a strange thing affects safety or money, we say so, briefly |
| **Recognition** | "هذا أنا بالضبط" (the work-stress outlier) | Second-person framing: "انت تسويها كل يوم" |
| **Send-to-someone** | The account's culture is "طرشووه" | Every episode has a named share target ("أرسلها للي دايماً يقول…") |

### 5. Brand Positioning

> **For** Arab viewers who scroll past trivia they forget in a minute,
> **غريب عجيب** is **the short documentary that reopens the everyday things
> you never questioned**,
> **told by** an Emirati architect who dissects ordinary things like buildings,
> **unlike** "هل تعلم" fact pages and fast explainers,
> **because** every episode is a verified story, with its source on screen and a
> reveal you can actually see.

**Where we sit on the map:**

SLOW ──────────────────┼────────────────── FAST
                       │

### 6. Core Promise

**في أقل من دقيقتين، بتشوف شي تعرفه طول عمرك… كأنك تشوفه لأول مرة. وكل
معلومة لها مصدر.**

In under two minutes you'll see something you've known your whole life as if
for the first time, and every claim has a source.

Three sub-promises the viewer can hold us to:

1. **One question, fully answered.** No cliffhanger bait, no "part 2".
2. **A reveal you see.** Not just hear.
3. **A source you can check.** On screen, in the title block, and in the caption.

## PART B · HOW WE TELL STORIES

### 7. Storytelling Philosophy

Seven rules:

1. **Story, not fact.** A fact is "the tongue is X cm". A story is "your body
   was built with a limit, and here's who first measured it." Every episode
   needs a **protagonist** (a person, an object, or the viewer's own body) and
   a **turn**.
2. **The viewer is the detective. Maryam holds the red pencil.** She doesn't
   lecture. She investigates *with* you and circles what matters.
3. **Show the answer.** If the voiceover could be removed and the reveal would
   still land, the episode is right. If not, redesign the visual.
4. **One question per episode.** If research uncovers two great questions,
   that's two episodes.
5. **Respect the belief before replacing it.** Grandmother's explanation gets
   screen time and dignity.
6. **Wonder over shock.** We never use "صدق أو لا تصدق", fake urgency, or
   horror framing.
7. **End on humanity.** The last thing the viewer feels is about people,
   themselves, or us. It's not a statistic.

**Maryam's voice on the show:** Emirati dialect, warm, curious, slightly
playful, never academic. She talks like the smartest friend at the table,
the one who went and actually looked it up. She uses "أنا وياكم" and "تخيلوا"
and never "أعزائي المتابعين".

### 8. Documentary Formula: **الملف** (The File)

Every episode is a **ملف** (case file). Seasons are **مجلدات** (volumes).
Episode numbering: **ملف ٠٠١**, **ملف ٠٠٢**… and it never resets across seasons.
Collectors remember numbers.

The formula in one line:

> **سؤال ← شائع ← شرخ ← ثلاث أدلة ← القَطع ← المعنى ← الختم**
> Question → Common belief → The crack → Three clues → The Cut → The meaning → The stamp

### 9. Episode Structure

Target runtime **60–90 seconds** (the standard file). A **ملف كبير** of 2–3
minutes is allowed once a month, once the standard file is proven.

| # | Beat | Arabic | Time (75s file) | Job | Camera |
| -: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The Question** | السؤال | 0:00–0:03 | The "ليش" question, spoken and on screen, with the object already moving | Desk cam: Maryam's hand places the object |
| 2 | **The Common Belief** | الشائع | 0:03–0:09 | What everyone thinks, or what grandmother said | Desk: collage of "the belief" |
| 3 | **The Crack** | الشرخ | 0:09–0:16 | The one detail that doesn't fit. Opens the loop. "بس… في شي ما يركب." | **Maryam to camera**, first appearance or reaction |
| 4 | **Clue 1** | الدليل ١ | 0:16–0:28 | First evidence: the micro-reveal | Desk |
| 5 | **Clue 2** | الدليل ٢ | 0:28–0:40 | Escalation: the mid-reveal | Desk |
| 6 | **Clue 3** | الدليل ٣ | 0:40–0:50 | The final piece: the setup for the cut | Desk, slow push-in |
| 7 | **The Cut** | القَطع | 0:50–0:60 | The answer, *seen*: section cut, red enters | Desk, the signature move |
| 8 | **The Meaning** | المعنى | 0:60–0:70 | Why this matters to you, the human turn | **Maryam to camera** |
| 9 | **The Stamp** | الختم | 0:70–0:75 | Closing line + stamp + "غريب… عجيب." + the door-question | Desk: stamp lands; loop frame |

**Hard rules:**
- The answer is never given before 60% of runtime.
- The first clue lands before 0:20, so the viewer gets paid early.
- Maryam's face appears **at least twice** (the Crack and the Meaning) and
  **at most 35%** of screen time.

### 10. Hook System

The hook is three hooks firing at once, and all three are designed separately:

| Layer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Spoken** | The "ليش" question or its variant, under 3 seconds, second person where possible |
| **On-screen** | ≤ 5 Arabic words, headline font, top third of the safe zone |
| **First frame** | Motion at frame 1: a hand, an object sliding in, paper being placed. Never a static face. Never a logo. |

**The 7 sanctioned hook types** (always pick 3, score them, shoot the best):

| # | Type | Pattern | Example from our own archive |
| -: | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | **The Impossible Question** | ليش ما تقدر…؟ | "ليش ما تقدر تلمس مرفقك بلسانك؟" |
| H2 | **The Familiar Flipped** | You do X every day. Do you know why? | "كل يوم تقول ألو… وما عمرك سألت من وين يات." |
| H3 | **Myth vs. Science** | They say X. Science says something else. | "طنين في أذنك؟ يقولون أحد يذكرك…" |
| H4 | **The Accusation** | You do this and you don't know it | "انت تسويها الحين، وانت تشوف هالفيديو." |
| H5 | **The Object** | Extreme close-up + "this has a secret" | "هالشي في بيت كل واحد فيكم… وله سر." |
| H6 | **The Number** | One verified, surprising figure | `{{verified number}}` + what it's about |
| H7 | **The Local Mystery** | Something specific to the Gulf | "ليش بيوت الفريج القديمة كانت…؟" `{{verify}}` |

**Banned openers:** greetings, "مرحبا يا جماعة", "في هذا الفيديو",
"هل تعلم", "صدق أو لا تصدق", "لازم تشوف هذا", "انتظر للنهاية", and the logo
or title card before the question.

### 11. Reveal System

**Three reveals per file, escalating:** micro at ~25% (Clue 1), mid at ~50%
(Clue 2), main at ~70% (The Cut).

**The grammar of the main reveal (القَطع, The Cut):**

1. **Silence.** Music drops out 0.5–0.8s before. Only paper and room tone remain.
2. **The Cut.** The signature move: a blade line draws across the object and
   the paper separates to show the **section**, the inside view, the way an
   architect draws a building cut in half.
3. **Red enters.** Correction Red appears for the first time in the episode
   (except in the hook pencil). Red = truth.
4. **One sentence.** The answer, ≤ 10 words, spoken *and* on screen.
5. **Hold 1.5 seconds.** Nothing moves except a slow push-in. Let it land.
6. **Music returns** on a new chord.

**Rules:**
- The main reveal must be **visual first, verbal second**.
- A reveal that needs a caveat gets the caveat *immediately* after, in the
  confidence language (section 28): "الأرجح…", "العلماء للحين مختلفين…"
- Never fake a reveal. If the honest answer is "nobody knows for sure", **that
  is the reveal**, and it's often the most powerful one.

### 12. Ending System

Every file ends in four moves, in this order, in under 10 seconds:

1. **The Meaning line.** Maryam, to camera, one sentence that turns the fact
   toward the viewer's life. ("يعني كل مرة تقول ألو… انت تكمل محادثة بدت قبل ١٥٠
   سنة." `{{verify}}`)
2. **The Stamp.** Hard cut to the desk. The **غريب عجيب** rubber stamp lands on
   the file. This is the sonic logo (section 23).
3. **The Signature.** Maryam's voice, off-screen: **"غريب… عجيب."** Always
   these two words, always this rhythm: a pause after غريب, and عجيب lands with
   the stamp.
4. **The Door.** One specific question for the comments, on screen, with no voiceover.
   It must be answerable from personal experience ("شو كانت يدتك تقول عن
   الطنين؟"), never "شو رايكم؟"

**The loop:** the final frame matches the composition of frame 1, so the
replay feels seamless.

**Never:** "تابعوني", "لا تنسون اللايك", "الجزء الثاني قريباً", or a
sponsor line in the ending.

## PART C · HOW IT LOOKS

### 13. Thumbnail System (frame 0 and the in-feed first impression)

The **thumbnail** is what a stranger sees before tapping: the frozen first
frame in Explore and on the Reels tab when autoplay is off. It's designed, not
grabbed.

**Frame 0 composition (1080×1920):**

```
┌──────────────────────────┐
│   [UI: top bar zone]     │  y 0–220   keep clear
│                          │
│     ليش نقول ألو؟         │  y 260–520 the question (≤5 words), headline font
│                          │
│   ┌──────────────────┐   │
│   │  THE OBJECT      │   │  y 560–1300 single object, cut-out on paper,
│   │  cut-out, with   │   │  dimension lines, a red pencil circle
│   │  red pencil mark │   │
│   └──────────────────┘   │
│ ┌────────┐               │
│ │ملف ٠٠٧ │  title block   │  y 1320–1420 file number
│ └────────┘               │
│   [UI: caption zone]     │  y 1440–1920 keep clear
└──────────────────────────┘
          ↑ right 230px clear for action rail
```

**Rules:** one object, one question, one red mark. The object is
photographed for real wherever possible, not stock. The question is the
episode title, word for word.

### 14. Cover System (grid, Highlights, season posters)

The profile grid shows a **3:4 crop** of the reel cover. Covers are designed so
the 3:4 center (1080×1440) works on its own *and* builds a recognizable row on
the grid.

**The standard cover:**
- **Background:** Paper (#F2EBDD) with the blueprint grid at 6% opacity.
- **Center:** the episode object as a paper cut-out, with a soft real shadow.
- **Top:** the question in 3–5 words, Ink color, with the key word circled in
  Correction Red by hand.
- **Bottom-right (RTL reading start):** **Paper Maryam**, a small black-and-white
  cut-out photo of Maryam holding the red pencil, *always the same size and
  position*. This is how the grid says "Maryam" before it says anything else.
- **Bottom-left:** the title block: **ملف ٠٠٧ · غريب عجيب**.

**Grid rhythm:** episodes are posted so that, when possible, covers sit in
consistent positions across rows. Non-show posts in between are expected.
The show covers should be the most consistent tiles on the grid.

**Other covers:**

| Asset | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| **Highlight cover** "غريب عجيب" | The stamp mark only, Ink on Paper |
| **Season poster** (مجلد ١) | All 8 objects of the season as a collage "evidence board", carousel cover |
| **Season trailer cover** (ملف ٠٠٠) | Paper Maryam full-size, red pencil, the stamp |
| **Carousel recap** of a file | Cover = same as reel cover; slides 2–6 = clues; last = sources |

### 15. Motion Graphics Style: **ورق ومسطرة** (Paper & Ruler)

Two vocabularies, one world: **the collage** (the story's emotion and history)
and **the drafting table** (the investigation's precision).

| Element | Purpose | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paper cut-outs** | Objects, people, places | Slide in with friction and a small overshoot; real drop shadow |
| **Dimension lines** | Measuring, comparing, scale | Draw on from both ends, Arabic labels, ticks at the ends |
| **Section cut** | The reveal | Blade line draws across; paper halves separate 30–60px |
| **Red pencil** | Attention, truth | Hand-drawn circles, underlines, arrows, always animated as a stroke, never popped |
| **Title block** | Source and file identity | Bottom corner of every key frame (see section 17) |
| **Tracing paper overlays** | "Before / after", "belief / truth" | Translucent sheet slides over, notes are written on it |
| **Stamps** | Verdicts and labels ("خرافة", "ثابت", "الأرجح") | Land with a thud, slight rotation (±3°), ink texture |
| **Handwriting** | Maryam's notes | Her actual handwriting, digitized (see section 16) |

**Never:** glows, lens flares, 3D extruded text, neon, glitch, liquid
transitions, emoji animations, or generic stock-template "kinetic typography".

### 16. Paper Collage Style

**The look:** an architect's desk crossed with a family archive. Precise lines on
top of warm, imperfect paper.

**Construction rules:**
1. **Everything is a physical-looking object** with thickness, a shadow, and
   an edge (cut or torn).
2. **Cut edges = facts. Torn edges = beliefs, memories, history.** Use this
   consistently and it becomes a language the audience reads without knowing it.
3. **Light comes from the top-right** (consistent shadow direction, matching an
   RTL reading start). Shadows are soft, 8–14px, 25–35% Ink.
4. **Maximum 4 depth layers** in any frame: background paper, main object,
   annotations, foreground hand/pencil.
5. **People are black-and-white photo cut-outs.** Color is reserved for objects
   and for Correction Red.
6. **Paper Maryam** is a recurring collage character: a B&W cut-out of Maryam
   in 6 poses (pointing, holding pencil, thinking, surprised, stamping,
   presenting). Shoot these once against a plain wall. They're used in covers,
   transitions and carousels.
7. **Maryam's handwriting becomes a font.** Scan 2 pages of her Arabic
   handwriting and digitize it. Use it for every annotation. Nobody else can
   own this.
8. **Scan your own textures** (section 20). Don't use the stock collage packs
   every other creator downloads.

### 17. Editorial Design Language

The show is designed like an **architectural drawing set inside a magazine**.

**Grid (1080×1920 frame):**
- Safe area: x 72–850, y 230–1440 (right 230px reserved for the action rail).
- 6 columns, 24px gutter, **8px baseline grid**.
- **RTL first:** reading, timelines, and "forward" motion all flow right → left.

**Layout types**, the only seven compositions we use:

| Code | Layout | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | **Plate** | One object, centered, dimension lines. Hook, cover. |
| L2 | **Section** | The cut view. Reveal only. |
| L3 | **Evidence Board** | 2–4 items pinned with masking tape. Clues. |
| L4 | **Timeline** | Right-to-left strip with dates. History. |
| L5 | **Quote Card** | Naskh quote on tracing paper + B&W portrait. A person speaks. |
| L6 | **Number Card** | One giant number + one line. Data. |
| L7 | **Belief vs. Truth** | Torn paper (belief) under tracing paper (truth). Myth episodes. |

**The Title Block**, our signature editorial device, borrowed from
architectural drawings. It sits bottom-left on every key frame:

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ غريب عجيب      │ ملف ٠٠٧       │
│ الموضوع: ألو                  │
│ المصدر: {{source short name}} │
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

It does three jobs: brand identity, episode identity, and **visible sourcing**.
No other Arabic creator puts the source *inside the frame*.

### 18. Typography Rules

| Role | Typeface (recommended) | Free alternative | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Headline / question** | A geometric Kufi display (e.g. *29LT Kaff* or a commissioned cut) | **Reem Kufi** Bold, or **Noto Kufi Arabic** Black | ≤ 5 words, never more than 2 lines, tracking 0, Ink or Paper only |
| **Subtitles / body** | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** SemiBold | same (free) | 2 lines max, ≤ 8 words a line, 54–60px, Paper with 85% Ink box |
| **Quotes / archive / Arabic heritage** | A classical Naskh (e.g. **Amiri**) | **Amiri** | Only on L5 Quote cards and historical documents |
| **Annotations** | **Maryam's handwriting font** (to be made) | Interim: **Aref Ruqaa** sparingly | Red or Ink only, always animated as written |
| **Technical labels** (dimensions, title block) | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** Regular, small caps-style spacing | same | 28–32px, Ink 80% |
| **Latin** (rare: brand names, scientific names) | **IBM Plex Sans** / **IBM Plex Mono** | same | Never larger than the Arabic beside it |

**Numerals:** use Western digits (0–9) for data and the Arabic-Indic digits
(٠–٩) only in the file number "ملف ٠٠٧", which is a brand mark. Never mix inside
one number. *(Challenge this if your audience testing says otherwise.)*

**Never:** more than 3 typefaces in one frame, outlined text, gradient text,
drop-shadowed text, stretched or squeezed Arabic, or a Latin font rendering Arabic.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `paper` | ورق · Paper | **#F2EBDD** | Base background | 50–70% |
| `ink` | حبر · Ink | **#1C1B19** | Type, lines, B&W photos | 15–25% |
| `kraft` | كرافت · Kraft | **#B98B5E** | Folders, envelopes, warmth | 5–15% |

**The Red Rule:** red means *this is true / this matters*. It appears in the
hook (the pencil mark), at the reveal, and on the stamp, and nowhere else. If
red is everywhere, it means nothing.

**Banned:** neon, gradients, pure white (#FFFFFF) backgrounds, pure black, and
the Instagram-purple/pink family.

### 21. Animation Language

- **Handmade timing.** Collage elements animate **on twos** (12 fps feel)
  inside a 30 fps timeline. Camera moves and pencil strokes animate on ones
  (smooth). The contrast reads as "made by hand, guided by a precise mind".
- **Physical easing.** Paper slides with friction: ease-out with a 2–4%
  overshoot and a settle. Nothing floats.
- **Direction carries meaning (RTL).** Right → left = forward, progress, the
  future. Left → right = the past, going back, the old belief.
- **Lines draw, they never appear.** Dimension lines, pencil circles and
  section cuts are always stroked on.
- **The camera is a person leaning over the desk.** Slow push-ins, gentle
  pans, never a spin, never a zoom-blur.
- **Stillness is a tool.** One held frame (1–1.5s) before and after each reveal.

### 22. Camera Language

**Two cameras define the show:**

| Camera | Setup | Role | Screen time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A · The Desk** | Top-down, 90°, locked, on the drafting table. 4K vertical. Soft, large window-style key from top-right, matching collage shadows | The world of the story: objects, paper, Maryam's hands, the red pencil, the stamp | 65–75% |
| **B · Maryam** | Medium close-up, eye level, 35–50mm equivalent, locked off, slow digital push-in allowed. Background: the **investigation wall** (pinned papers, blueprint, the season's objects), softly out of focus | The host: the Crack, the Meaning, reactions | 25–35% |

**Rules:**
- No handheld. No gimbal walk-and-talks inside the show (keep those for other content).
- **Maryam's hands are a character:** same red pencil every episode, the same
  ring or bracelet if she wears one, sleeves in neutral tones.
- **Wardrobe:** neutral palette (Ink, Paper, Kraft, deep blue). Red never on
  wardrobe, because red is truth.
- **Match cuts** between cameras: an object leaves Maryam's hand on camera B and
  lands on the desk in camera A.
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps capture; 60 fps for any physical "slow" demo.

### 23. Sound Identity

**The sonic logo: "The Stamp".** About 1.2 seconds:
1. Paper slide (0.3s)
2. Rubber stamp thud, felt in the chest (0.2s)
3. A two-note motif: **غريب** on a rising oud harmonic, **عجيب** on a low
   warm note under the thud.

It plays at the end of every file. It also opens the season trailer, and
nowhere else. Scarcity keeps it valuable.

**The Foley palette** (the show is *heard* as paper):
pencil scratch, paper slide, page flip, tape tear, ruler click, scissors
snip, folder open, stamp thud, and a room-tone "studio air" bed.

**Voice:** Maryam's voiceover recorded close-mic in a treated space, warm
and present, gently compressed. Same mic, same room, every episode. Voice is 90% of
brand recognition with eyes closed.

### 24. Music Direction

- **Character:** curious, intimate, slightly mischievous. A detective walking
  through a museum at night.
- **Palette:** pizzicato strings, muted piano, oud harmonics and short plucks,
  soft frame-drum or daf pulses, low synth pad for tension. No EDM drops, no
  cinematic "BRAAAM", no generic corporate ukulele.
- **Tempo:** 88–100 BPM for investigation, half-time feel for the Meaning.
- **Structure follows the formula:** the Question gets a sting, the Belief
  a warm motif, the Crack a tension pad, the Clues a rising pulse, the Cut
  **silence**, the Meaning a resolved warm chord, the Stamp the sonic logo.
- **Ownership:** commission a **غريب عجيب theme in 3 stems** (pulse,
  melody, pad) from a Gulf composer, plus 6 cues. Until then, use one licensed
  library and a fixed shortlist of 10 tracks.
- **Trending audio is not used inside the show.** It trades brand memory for
  a one-off reach bump. (See the challenge in part 4; this is a deliberate choice.)

### 25. Editing Rhythm

**The tempo curve of a file:**

```
intensity
   ▲
   │ ██                                   ██
   │ ██      ▄▄       ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄            ██
   │ ██ ▄▄  ████   ▄▄████████     ▄▄▄▄    ██
   │ ████████████████████████  ·  ████████████
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────▶ time
     Q  Belief Crack  Clues 1-2-3   Cut  Meaning Stamp
                                    ↑ silence
```

- **Visual change every 1.5–2.5 s** in the Clues; never more than **4 s**
  without a new element, move or annotation.
- **Hook:** no cut in the first 1.5 s. The motion lives *inside* the frame.
- **Cut on consonants, not on breaths.** Remove breaths except before the reveal.
- **J-cuts** (sound before picture) into every new clue.
- **One breath:** a 1–1.5 s held beat before the Cut. This is the only planned
  silence and it's sacred.
- **Speech pace:** natural, not rushed. Measure Maryam's Arabic words per
  minute on the first 3 files and set the script length from it. Don't guess.

### 26. Transition System

**Only five transitions exist in the show:**

| Code | Transition | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | **The Slide**: new paper slides in from the right and covers the frame | Default between scenes |
| T2 | **The Tear**: the current paper is torn away, revealing the next | Belief → investigation; past → present |
| T3 | **The Flip**: a folder page flips (RTL direction) | Between Clues |
| T4 | **The Match**: the object moves from Maryam's hand (B) to the desk (A) | Between cameras |
| T5 | **The Cut**: the section cut | **The reveal only, once per episode** |

**Banned:** whip pans, zoom blur, glitch, spin, cube, light leaks, flash
frames, and any transition from a template pack.

### 27. Research Workflow

| Step | What | Output | Time box |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | **Question lock.** One "ليش" question, written as Maryam would say it | The question, 1 line | 15 min |
| R2 | **Lead sweep.** Encyclopedias, review articles, university pages, books. AI tools may help *find leads only* | 8–15 leads | 45 min |
| R3 | **Source pull.** Go to the primary or Tier-1 source for every lead you keep | Source list with links and page numbers | 90 min |
| R4 | **Story mining.** Find the *person* (who discovered, named, built, believed), the *turn*, and the *Arab or local angle* | Story notes | 45 min |
| R5 | **Visual research.** What can be shown? What objects can we film? What archival images can we clear? | Visual list with rights status | 45 min |
| R6 | **Expert touch** (when the topic is medical, historical-contested, or technical) | One expert email or call, quoted with permission | as needed |
| R7 | **Research brief.** Fill the Research Template | `research.md` | 30 min |

### 28. Fact-Checking Workflow

**Source tiers:**

| Tier | What counts | How we use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1** | Peer-reviewed papers, systematic reviews, primary documents, official archives (incl. UAE National Archives), museum collections, standards bodies | Can carry a claim alone if it's directly on point |
| **T2** | Major encyclopedias, university and hospital pages, reputable newspapers of record, books from academic presses | Needs a second independent source |
| **T3** | **Never cited:** other reels, fact pages, content farms, "هل تعلم" sites, forum posts, AI chatbot output, uncredited infographics | Use only to *find* T1/T2 sources |

**The rules:**
1. **Every on-screen or spoken claim** goes into the **Claim Ledger** (in the
   Research Template) with its ID, the script line, the source, the exact
   supporting quote, and a confidence grade.
2. **Two independent sources** per claim, at least one T1 or T2.
3. **The confidence language.** Maryam's wording must match the grade:

   | Grade | Meaning | Spoken as |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | **ثابت** (Established) | Scientific or historical consensus | stated plainly |
   | **الأرجح** (Most likely) | Strong evidence, some debate | "الأرجح إن…", "أغلب العلماء يقولون…" |
   | **مختلف عليه** (Contested) | Real disagreement | "في رأيين…", "للحين ما في جواب أكيد" |
   | **غير مؤكد** (Unverified) | Can't be confirmed | **not used in the episode** |

4. **Numbers are checked twice**, against the source and by a second person.
5. **Etymology and "first ever" claims are the most dangerous.** Treat them as
   contested until proven otherwise.
6. **The fact-checker is not the writer.** If the team is one person, the check
   happens 24 hours after writing, from the ledger, line by line.
7. **Image and footage rights** are checked in the same pass. Every asset is
   own / licensed / public domain / permitted, with proof.
8. **Corrections policy:** an error found after publishing gets a pinned
   comment "تصحيح" within 24 hours, the caption is updated, and a 5-second
   correction appears at the start of the next file. Owning mistakes publicly
   builds more trust than never making them.

### 29. Topic Selection Rules

**The four pillars** (rotate; no pillar twice in a row):

| Pillar | Arabic | Examples from the archive and the direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 · Your strange body** | جسمك الغريب | Elbow and tongue, static shock, ear ringing, laughing when sleepy |
| **P2 · Our habits and words** | عاداتنا وكلامنا | ألو, why the oud means "the past", why we say what we say |
| **P3 · Things and places** (the architect's pillar) | أشياء وأماكن | Why buildings, objects and cities are the way they are. **This is our moat.** |
| **P4 · Strange stories of our region** | غرائب منطقتنا | Gulf and Arab history's strange, verifiable moments |

**The Greenlight Score.** Every topic is scored 1–5 on six criteria. **Greenlight
at ≥ 22 / 30, with no criterion below 3.**

| Criterion | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Familiarity** | Does almost everyone in our audience do, see or say this? |
| **Strangeness gap** | Is the true answer genuinely surprising? |
| **Visual potential** | Can we *show* the answer: cut it, collage it, measure it? |
| **Verifiability** | Do two independent T1/T2 sources exist? |
| **Majlis retellability** | Can a viewer retell it in two sentences tonight? |
| **Us-ness** | Does it connect to Arab/Gulf life, language or people? |

### 30. Things We Never Cover

1. **Religious rulings, fatwas, or interpreting religious texts.** Faith is
   respected, never used as evidence or debated.
2. **The unseen presented as real or unreal:** جن, حسد, black magic. Too
   sensitive in both directions. We stay out.
3. **Politics, conflicts, governments' disputes, sectarian topics.** (Maryam
   covers national and humanitarian themes on her main content. The show stays neutral ground.)
4. **Medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, supplements, "health hacks".** Body
   episodes explain; they never prescribe. If relevant: "إذا يصير لك وايد، راجع
   الطبيب."
5. **Conspiracy theories as if they could be true.** We only debunk a belief if
   it's widespread *and* harmless to discuss.
6. **Mocking any nationality, tribe, dialect, region, or body.**
7. **Private individuals' tragedies**, and children without guardian consent.
8. **Unverifiable viral "facts"**, however good they sound.
9. **Sponsored topics disguised as episodes.** (Rules in part 4, challenge 4.)
10. **Shock for its own sake:** gore, disaster footage, fear framing.

### 31. Quality Control Checklist (the gate before publishing)

A file publishes only when every box is ticked. The full version lives in
`templates/editing-checklist.md` and `templates/publishing-checklist.md`.
These are the **seven non-negotiables**:

1. **The Majlis Test:** someone outside the team retells the episode in two
   sentences after one watch.
2. **The Mute Test:** watched muted, the story still makes sense from visuals
   and subtitles.
3. **The Blur Test:** with Maryam's face blurred and sound off, it's
   recognizably غريب عجيب within 2 seconds (paper, red pencil, title block).
4. **The Ledger Test:** every claim in the final cut is in the Claim Ledger as
   ثابت or الأرجح, and worded to match.
5. **The Red Test:** red appears only in the hook mark, the reveal, and the stamp.
6. **The Hook Test:** question spoken by 0:03, on screen at frame 1, motion at frame 1.
7. **The Respect Test:** would the grandmother whose belief we investigated feel
   respected watching it?

### 32. Brand Memory Strategy

**Goal:** people remember **Maryam and غريب عجيب** before they remember the fact.

We build **distinctive brand assets**: things that, seen or heard alone,
mean "this is غريب عجيب". They only work through **repetition without
variation**. **No asset changes for the first 20 files.**

| # | Asset | Sense | Where it appears |
| -: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **"ليش…؟"**: every title is a "why" question | Words | Title, hook, cover |
| 2 | **The red pencil** in Maryam's hand | Sight | Hook, reveal, covers |
| 3 | **The Stamp**: sound + visual | Sound + sight | End of every file |
| 4 | **"غريب… عجيب."**: the spoken signature | Voice | End of every file |
| 5 | **The Title Block** with file number and source | Sight | Every key frame |
| 6 | **Paper Maryam**: her B&W cut-out | Sight | Covers, transitions |
| 7 | **The Red Rule**: red only means truth | Sight | Whole show |
| 8 | **Maryam's handwriting** | Sight | Annotations |
| 9 | **The file numbers**: ملف ٠٠١ → | Words | Everywhere; builds the "collection" instinct |

**Memory mechanics:**
- **Fixed slot:** same day and time each week, so the habit forms.
- **Season trailer (ملف ٠٠٠):** 30 seconds that introduce the assets
  *before* Episode 1, so Episode 1 already feels like a show.
- **Highlights archive** "غريب عجيب" with every file and the season posters.
- **Viewers name the next file.** At the end of each season, the community
  sends questions and the best becomes a file credited to them. This creates
  ownership and recurring comments.
- **Measure it:** after file 8 and file 16, run a story poll with a frame of
  paper, a red circle and a stamp, but no name, asking "مين يسوي هالفيديوهات؟".
  Recognition rate is the brand KPI.
