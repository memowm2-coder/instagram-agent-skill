## The show in nine lines

1. **Promise:** something you've known your whole life, seen for the first time, with a source.
2. **Format:** ملف (file), 60–90 s, numbered forever: ملف ٠٠١ →
3. **Formula:** سؤال ← شائع ← شرخ ← ثلاث أدلة ← القَطع ← المعنى ← الختم
4. **Look:** ورق ومسطرة (Paper & Ruler): collage warmth + architect's drafting precision.
5. **The Red Rule:** red means truth, and appears only at the hook mark, the reveal and the stamp.
6. **The signature:** the stamp lands, Maryam says **"غريب… عجيب."**
7. **Truth:** two sources per claim, confidence language, source in the frame.
8. **Pillars:** جسمك الغريب · عاداتنا وكلامنا · أشياء وأماكن · غرائب منطقتنا
9. **Cadence:** seasons of 8, three files banked before launch, a fixed weekly slot.
