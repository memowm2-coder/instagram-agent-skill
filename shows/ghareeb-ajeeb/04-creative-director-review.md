# 04 · Creative Director's Review

**غريب عجيب · Maryam Studios · Show Bible, part 4 of 4**

You asked me not to agree automatically. I haven't. Here's where I'd push
back on the brief, on the current show, and on some instincts I'd expect
you to have.

---

## The pitch in one breath

> **غريب عجيب** is a weekly short documentary where an Emirati architect takes
> something you do every day (a word, a habit, an object, a building) and cuts
> it open in front of you. Every file is one question, three clues, one reveal
> you can *see*, and a source you can check. It looks like an architect's desk
> crossed with a family archive. It sounds like paper and a pencil. It ends,
> every time, with a stamp and two words: **غريب… عجيب.**

---

## Ten challenges

### 1. The show you have now is a trivia format, and your audience isn't a trivia audience.
Your six episodes sit at your account median. Your outliers (229K on a
child's poem about the mother, 6.4K on a protective warning, 2.4K on work
stress) are **emotion, protection, recognition, identity**.
**My call:** keep the "ليش" question as the door and change what's behind it.
Every file now ends on **المعنى** (the human turn). If a topic has no human turn,
it isn't a غريب عجيب topic.

### 2. You can't own the words "غريب عجيب".
It's an everyday phrase and the hashtag belongs to everyone. (One of your
own posts even tagged #غرييب_عجيب.) Renaming would throw away six episodes of
equity and a name that's warm and sayable.
**My call:** keep the name, and own it through **assets, not words**: the stamp,
the red pencil, the title block, Paper Maryam, "غريب… عجيب." with the pause.
For the English name, transliterate as **Ghareeb Ajeeb** rather than
"Strange & Amazing", which is generic in English.

### 3. Paper collage alone is a trend, not an identity.
Half of design-led short-form uses torn paper right now. If that's our whole
look, we're a template.
**My call:** the moat is **you are an architect**. Section cuts, dimension lines,
title blocks, plans and elevations are a visual language no Arabic documentary
creator uses, and it's *true to you*. Collage gives warmth; drafting gives
authority. P3 (things and places) gets 3 of every 8 files for the same reason.

### 4. Sponsorship will try to eat the show. Decide now.
Your feed is largely ministries, campaigns and partners. The moment غريب عجيب
works, it becomes the most sponsorable thing you own, and the first
disguised ad will spend the trust it took a season to build.
**My call, to approve or reject:**
- A partner can **present a season** ("مجلد ١ بدعم من…"), shown on the
  trailer and season poster only.
- A partner **never** chooses a topic, edits the answer, or appears inside a file.
- A client-requested topic becomes a **"ملف خاص"**, clearly labeled and
  disclosed, and it still has to pass the Greenlight Score and the full fact-check.
- No sponsored post within 24 hours either side of a file.

### 5. "Remember Maryam before the information" is right, but not by putting your face everywhere.
Too much face and it's a vlog. Too little and it's a faceless channel that
anyone could clone.
**My call:** you're present through **hands, pencil, handwriting, voice and
Paper Maryam** the whole time, and on camera for exactly the moments that need a
human: the Crack and the Meaning. That's 25–35% face, 100% Maryam.

### 6. The show is fighting your own feed.
A documentary between an event recap and a campaign reel reads as "another
post". Netflix doesn't air its flagship doc between ads with no frame around it.
**My call:** don't split into a separate account yet (you'd give up 56K
reach for purity). Instead: fixed slot, unmistakable covers, a pinned season
trailer, a Highlight, and the 24-hour sponsor buffer. **Revisit at file 16**:
if the show's followers clearly differ from the main account's, a dedicated
account becomes worth it.

### 7. "Netflix quality" is a cadence trap.
The pipeline is about 25–30 hours per file. Weekly, with a small team, alongside
event hosting and client work, will break by file 5, and an irregular show
has no habit (your last 6 episodes had gaps of up to 9 months).
**My call:** seasons of **8 files**, **bank 3 finished files before launch**,
two-week breaks between seasons. A smaller show that always shows up beats
a perfect show that disappears.

### 8. Don't start long. Earn long.
Documentary ambition pulls toward 2–3 minutes. Start at **60–90 seconds**,
prove the formula holds attention, then allow one **ملف كبير** a month.

### 9. Trending audio: I'm saying no, and you might disagree.
Trending sounds can lift reach. They also make every file sound like everyone
else's. A documentary brand is built on its own sound.
**My call:** no trending audio inside the show. If you want the reach, use it
in the **story teasers** and the **season trailer cut-downs**, never in the file.

### 10. Launch with File 000, not File 001.
If Episode 1 has to introduce the stamp, the pencil, the title block and the
formula *and* tell a story, it'll do neither well.
**My call:** a **30-second season trailer (ملف ٠٠٠)** first. Paper Maryam,
the desk, the red pencil, eight objects of the season flashing past, the
stamp, "غريب… عجيب." Episode 1 then arrives already feeling like a show.

## Improvements I'd make beyond the brief

1. **Digitize your handwriting** into a font. It's the most ownable asset
   in this whole Bible, and it takes an afternoon.
2. **Commission the theme.** Three stems and six cues from a Gulf composer.
   The sonic logo is worth more than any visual effect.
3. **Build the texture library from real Emirati paper:** old school
   notebooks, receipts, local newspapers you have rights to scan. Nobody can
   download it.
4. **Source on screen.** The title block puts the source *inside the frame*.
   In a region full of unverified "هل تعلم" pages, visible sourcing is
   positioning, not a footnote.
5. **The corrections policy is a feature.** The first time you publicly
   correct a mistake, you'll gain more trust than you lose.
6. **Let the audience write season 2.** End season 1 with a call for "ليش"
   questions. The best become files credited to the viewer by name.
7. **Arabic tooling.** This repo's hook scorer, beat timer and humanizer are
   English-tuned. I'd build Arabic versions (dialect-aware WPM timing, Arabic
   hook checks, an Arabic slop lexicon) before season 1 production. I've left
   them out until you approve the Bible.
