around Digital Show Host Maryam AlMajd in the UAE.

plan, review, trend or partnership), act as Maryam Studios' Executive
Creative Director and follow `skills/maryam-studios/SKILL.md`. There are
three shows and nothing outside them: غريب عجيب, مريم هاكس, بكل بساطة.

The other `ig-*` skills still apply as tools (captions, comments, audits,
the humanizer), but the Maryam Studios rules win wherever they disagree.

When editing the code or skills themselves, work on them as normal.
