# The three shows

The bibles. Each show has fixed parts and free parts. The fixed parts are the
brand: change them and the audience stops recognising the show. The free parts
are where the creativity goes.

---

## §1 غريب عجيب

**Job:** reach. Virality, curiosity, storytelling.
**The viewer says:** "مستحييييل"
**It is:** a documentary mystery. **It is not:** an educational show.

Every episode starts with something people already know, then reveals
something they never knew. Every episode answers **ليش؟** But never right
away. Build the question, make them wait, then pay them back.

### Production format (fixed)

- **Motion graphics and Maryam's voice. Nothing else.** Maryam never appears
  on camera in غريب عجيب, not even for one line. Her voice is her face here.
- Every frame is built in the collage world (`visual-language.md`): archive
  paper, cutouts, maps, typewriter labels, stop-motion on twos.
- Footage, if any, is licensed or archival and treated as a cutout inside the
  collage, never full-frame raw video.
- The voice is recorded clean, close and intimate, like telling a secret. The
  pace slows into the reveal and the silence before it is part of the edit.

### Structure (fixed)

| tag | beat | target | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `[INTERRUPT]` | Pattern interrupt | 0-2s | A familiar thing seen wrong. The image does the work before a word lands. |
| `[QUESTION]` | The question | 2-5s | The ليش؟ the viewer has never asked and now cannot stop asking. |
| `[MYSTERY]` | The mystery | 5-12s | The wrong answers everyone assumes. Make it stranger, not clearer. |
| `[STORY]` | The story | 12-25s | A person, a place, a year, a moment. The facts live inside it. |
| `[REVEAL]` | The reveal | 25-32s | The answer, in one sentence a child could repeat. |
| `[EXPLAIN]` | Verified explanation | 32-40s | Why it is true. The source appears on screen as an archive label. |
| `[TWIST]` | Unexpected twist | 40-46s | One more thing that reframes the whole story. The reason people share it. |
| `[END]` | Ending | last 2-4s | Close the loop on the opening image. Maryam's line, then the stamp. |

**Length:** 30-60s, ideal 35-50s. Never pad to reach a number.

### Free parts

The topic, the story, the twist, Maryam's closing line. Topics that work:
everyday objects, UAE places and history, aviation and travel (everyone here
flies), food, words and names, money, the body, cities, the desert and the sea.

### Rules

- The first frame is an image, not Maryam saying hello.
- No fact without a story around it. "In 1971..." is a fact. "In 1971, a man
  standing on this exact beach..." is a story.
- The reveal is one sentence. If it needs two, the story is not finished.
- The twist is true and sourced like everything else. A twist you cannot
  verify is cut, not softened.
- Maryam is the guide, not the narrator of a Wikipedia page. She is allowed to
  be surprised, to doubt, to say "أنا بعد ما صدقت".

### Example (structure, not a final script; verify before shooting)

```
[INTERRUPT]  (Night flight, cabin lights cut to black. Paper-rip.)
مريم: كل مرة تنزل الطيارة بالليل... يطفون الأنوار.
[QUESTION]
مريم: ليش؟ إنت في أخطر دقايق الرحلة... وهم يطفون النور؟
[MYSTERY]
مريم: قالوا عشان الكهربا. قالوا عشان ترتاح. ولا وحدة صح.
[STORY]
مريم: تخيل الطيارة وقفت فجأة وصار لازم الكل يطلع... بالليل.
مريم: عيونك تحتاج وقت لين تتعود على الظلام. وهالوقت ما عندك إياه.
[REVEAL]
مريم: فيطفون النور قبل... عشان عيونك تكون جاهزة للظلام برا.
[EXPLAIN]   (Archive label on screen: {{source, e.g. aviation safety authority}})
مريم: إذا صار طارئ، تشوف الطريق والمخرج من أول ثانية.
[TWIST]
مريم: ونفس السبب... يطلبون منك تفتح الشباك وقت الإقلاع والهبوط.
مريم: مب عشان المنظر. عشان إذا صار شي برا... أحد يشوفه.
[END]
مريم: المرة الجاية لين يطفون النور... إنت تعرف ليش.
(Stamp: غريب عجيب)

source: {{تحقق: official aviation safety source}}
```

## §2 مريم هاكس

**Job:** the relationship. People come back for Maryam, not for the hack.
**The viewer says:** "ههههه... حفظتها"

### The fixed lines (never change a word)

Opening, every episode:

```
صوت:  مريم... مريم...
مريم: هلا.
صوت:  ممكن تعطينا هاك اليوم؟
```

The rush, every episode:

```
صوت:  بسرعة
مريم: يلا تم.
```

Ending, every episode:

```
مريم: عطيتكم الهاك...
      والباقي عليكم...
      يلا باي.
```

These lines are the show. Delivery can vary (tired, whispering, mouth full),
the words cannot.

### Production format (fixed)

- **Maryam on camera, face to camera.** This is the show where people see
  her. Real footage, never collage-only.
- **The calling voice is AI-generated**, one voice designed once and reused in
  every episode. Never seen, never changed. Rules:
  - a voice designed for us, never a clone of a real person's voice;
  - keep the voice tool's licence for commercial use on file;
  - mark the post with Instagram's AI label (the "AI info" disclosure for
    realistic AI-generated audio). It costs nothing and protects the brand.
- **The hack itself** is shown with: an iPhone mockup (motion graphics),
  real screen recordings of the steps inside the mockup, B-roll of hands on a
  real iPhone, and motion-graphic callouts (tap circles, arrows, zooms) in the
  sticky-note world.
- **The hacks are the newest and strongest iPhone tricks**, ideally from the
  current iOS release. Before an episode is written, the trick is tested on a
  device running the latest iOS, and the exact menu path is written down from
  that device. A trick that is older than two iOS versions needs a reason
  (hidden, still unknown, big payoff) or it goes to the backup bank.
- Screen recordings: clean device, no personal notifications, no real
  contacts or messages on screen, Do Not Disturb on.
- **iPhone language: English** (decided 10 Oct). Most people in the UAE run
  their phone in English. Every button Maryam names gets an on-screen card
  with the English label and its Arabic meaning, e.g.
  `Save Video Frame as Photo = احفظ اللقطة كصورة`.

### Structure

| tag | beat | target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[OPEN]` | Maryam busy with the gag + "مريم... مريم..." / "هلا." / "ممكن تعطينا هاك اليوم؟" | 0-4s |
| `[RUSH]` | "بسرعة" / "يلا تم." | 4-6s |
| `[HACK]` | The hack, shown, not explained. One hack only. | 6-22s |
| `[PROOF]` | The result on screen. | 22-26s |
| `[CLOSE]` | "عطيتكم الهاك... والباقي عليكم... يلا باي." and back to the gag | last 4s |

**Length:** 15-35s. A hack that needs 45s is a بكل بساطة episode.

### The gag (free, and it is the whole point)

Maryam is always busy doing something funny when the voice calls her. The
gag runs under the opening and comes back in the last frame, slightly worse.

Running list, never repeat one inside 8 episodes:
working on a laptop that is closed · studying with the book upside down ·
in a "meeting" alone · cooking with no stove on · talking on a phone that is
clearly off · sipping from an empty coffee cup, again · cleaning one spot for
the whole episode · gym, holding the dumbbell, not lifting it · ironing the
same shirt · reading a newspaper from 2019 · stuck in a parking ticket
machine · drinking karak that is way too hot.

### Retention note

The opening is about 4 seconds before the hack starts. That only works
because the first frame is already funny. The gag must be visible and moving
at frame 1, and the on-screen card at frame 1 states the hack's payoff
(`ملف PDF بدون تطبيق`). The words stay fixed. The frame does the hooking.

### Example

The locked pilot is `episodes/2026-11/05-thu-hack-video-frame.txt`: an iOS 27
trick (save any frame of a video as a full-quality photo), Maryam typing on a
closed laptop as the gag.

## §3 بكل بساطة

**Job:** help people living in the UAE, citizens and residents. Explain life
simply, warn them early, save them money and time.
**The viewer says:** "الحين فهمت."
**It is not:** news, announcements, or government PR.

Government entities should watch it and think: "we want Maryam to explain our
service." They think that because Maryam is clearer than their own page, not
because she repeats it.

### Three episode types

| type | what it is | example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **تنبيه** (alert) | something that can cost the viewer money, a fine, or their data, right now | the fake traffic-fine message |
| **نصيحة** (tip) | something that saves money or time that most people do not know | a discount, a free service, a shortcut in a government app |
| **خدمة** (service) | a service explained so simply that the viewer can do it today | the train from Dubai to Abu Dhabi |

The bar for every episode: a script that people send to their family group.
If nobody would forward it, it is not a بكل بساطة episode yet.

### Production format (fixed)

- **Maryam face to camera** for the hook, the key warning and the close.
- **B-roll** for everything she explains: real places, real phones, real
  apps (screen recordings), rebuilt in blueprint style where the real screen
  is cluttered.
- Blueprint motion graphics for steps, numbers and the catch.

### Topics

Government services · apps · technology · business · housing · healthcare ·
education · money and banks · AI · transport · rules · opportunities.
Anything that makes life here easier.

### Structure

| tag | beat | target | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `[HOOK]` | The hook, face to camera | 0-3s | The alert, the money, or the moment of confusion everyone has had. Never the entity's name. |
| `[STAKE]` | What it costs you | 3-8s | Why this matters to the viewer today: the money, the fine, the time, the risk. |
| `[STEPS]` | The explanation | 6-35s | Steps as a blueprint. One idea per beat. Plain words, no jargon. |
| `[CATCH]` | The thing nobody tells you | 35-42s | The fee, the deadline, the mistake people make. Sourced. |
| `[END]` | Close | last 3s | Maryam: "بكل بساطة." Stamp. |

**Length:** 30-60s. If it needs more, split it into a numbered series.

### Rules

- Open on the viewer's problem, never on the entity's name or logo.
- Every number (fee, days, age, amount) is sourced and dated. Unsourced
  numbers ship as `{{تحقق: ...}}` and the episode is not approved until they
  are filled.
- No PR language: "في إطار", "تماشياً مع", "يسرنا", "بهدف تعزيز". Say what it
  does for the person.
- Show the real app or form screens when possible, rebuilt in blueprint style
  if they are cluttered. Never show a viewer's personal data.
- Maryam can say something is confusing. That honesty is the brand.

### Example (structure only; every step must be checked in the live app)

```
[HOOK]  (Face to camera, holding a pile of paper forms)
مريم: كل معاملة تبي منك نفس الأوراق... كل مرة.
[STAKE]
مريم: بكل بساطة... الحل في تطبيق واحد. UAE PASS.
[STEPS]    (Blueprint: one phone, lines out to service logos)
مريم: هو هويتك الرقمية. حساب واحد تدخل فيه على خدمات حكومية كثيرة.
مريم: ومنه توقّع أوراقك رقمياً... بدون ما تطبع ولا تروح.
مريم: {{تحقق: خطوات التسجيل كما في التطبيق الحالي}}
[CATCH]
مريم: {{تحقق: الشي اللي الناس تغلط فيه}}
[END]
مريم: بكل بساطة.

source: {{تحقق: official UAE PASS page}} (checked {{date}})
```
