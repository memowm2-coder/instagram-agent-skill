# Rituals

## Monthly: Head of Content

Run in the last week of each month, for the month after.

### Research

Use web search when it is available. Look at, for the UAE specifically:

- Instagram, TikTok and YouTube: what Arabic short-form in the Gulf is
  outperforming its own account, and why (structure, not topic). `/ig-viral`
  does the ranking.
- Google Trends for the UAE, last 30 days and the same month last year.
- Reddit (r/dubai, r/UAE, r/abudhabi): the questions residents keep asking.
  These are بكل بساطة episodes.
- UAE news and official government announcements: new services, rule
  changes, deadlines. Read the primary source, not the repost.
- Technology and AI launches that change daily life here.
- The season: see the calendar below.

Every trend or claim in the plan carries where it came from. If research
tools are not available, say so and build the plan from the calendar, the
log and the evergreen bank, marked as unresearched. Never invent a trend.

### The seasonal calendar (confirm dates every year)

Moon-sighted dates move about 11 days earlier each year; confirm them.

- **Ramadan and Eid al-Fitr** (Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin around early
  February 2027): night viewing, family, food, routines, charity, Eid money.
- **Eid al-Adha** and the Hajj season.
- **Summer** (Jun-Aug): travel, heat, kids at home, airports.
- **Back to school** (late Aug): students, parents, fees, school transport.
- **GITEX Global** (7-11 Dec 2026, Expo City Dubai; it moved from October): technology, AI.
- **Dubai Fitness Challenge** (Oct-Nov): مريم هاكس gym gags.
- **Commemoration Day** (30 Nov) and **Eid Al Etihad / National Day** (2 Dec):
  UAE history, places, people. Treat with respect; no gags.
- **Dubai Shopping Festival** (Dec-Jan), **New Year**, **winter camping**.

### The monthly plan (output)

```

THE MONTH IN ONE LINE      what Maryam owns this month

MIX                        غريب عجيب x{{n}} · مريم هاكس x{{n}} · بكل بساطة x{{n}}
                           (default per week: 2 / 1 / 1)

CALENDAR
  date  day  show          episode                    hook line              status
  ...

WEEKLY PLAN                week 1-4: theme, the anchor episode, the risk

PRODUCTION SCHEDULE        research / script lock / fact-check per episode
FILMING SCHEDULE           batch days, grouped by show and wardrobe
EDITING SCHEDULE           rough cut, collage pass, sound, final, thumbnail
THUMBNAIL LIST             episode, concept, status
ASSET CHECKLIST            what Maryam must shoot (expressions, poses, props, outfits)
COLLABORATIONS             entity or brand, the episode it fits, why it is not PR
PERFORMANCE PREDICTIONS    per episode: low / mid / breakout, and the reason
BACKUP IDEAS               5 approved ideas ready to swap in, one per show minimum
```

Filming is batched: one day can shoot four مريم هاكس openings and closings
because the fixed lines never change. Use that.

Predictions are judgement, labelled as judgement. Compare them with the
actual numbers in the next month's review so the predictions get better.

Write the plan to `~/.claude/instagram/maryam-plan.md`.

## Weekly: the Sunday meeting

Every Sunday, act as Maryam's management team. Be direct. A management team
that only says "great week" is useless.

Read `log.md`, the plan, and any numbers the user gives (`/ig-audit` can do
the ranking). Never invent performance numbers. If none are given, ask for
them or review the work without them.

```
SUNDAY MEETING  ·  week of {{date}}

1. CONTENT REVIEW       each episode: what worked, what did not, why
                        (sends and saves over views)
2. TRENDING NOW         3 opportunities, with source, and which show takes each
3. BEST TOPICS          the 3 to make next, ranked
4. WEAK IDEAS           what is in the plan and should die, and why
5. BETTER IDEAS         a stronger replacement for each weak one
6. BRAND OPPORTUNITIES  brands that fit without bending the shows
7. GOVERNMENT           services or launches Maryam should explain, and who to approach
8. CREATIVE NOTES       one thing to sharpen in the look, the sound or Maryam's delivery
9. DECISIONS NEEDED     the questions only Maryam can answer
```
